# Privacy policy — Simple ADB

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Simple ADB (package com.eclipse.simpleadb) and its watch companion Simple ADB Helper (com.eclipse.simpleadb.helper) are made by Eclipse (Squad1996).

## What we collect
Nothing. Simple ADB has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting service. We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal or device data.

## What the app does with data on your devices
- Simple ADB connects your phone to your own Wear OS watch, over your local Wi-Fi (Android's Wireless debugging) or over Bluetooth (Simple ADB Helper). Commands, files, screenshots, screen recordings and the live screen go directly between your phone and your watch. They never pass through our servers; we have none.
- Files you download from the watch, screenshots and recordings are saved on your phone, in places you choose. You can delete them at any time.
- The app keeps a list of your paired watches, saved commands, routines and its own logs on your phone only. Uninstalling the app deletes them.
- Logs and diagnostics are only shared if you tap Copy or Share yourself, and only with the app you choose.

## Permissions
- Network and Wi-Fi: to find and connect to your watch on your own network.
- Bluetooth (Nearby devices): to talk to Simple ADB Helper on your paired watch.
- Nearby Wi-Fi devices / location on Android 12 and older: only for the optional Wi-Fi Direct speed test between your phone and watch. Simple ADB does not use or store your location.
- Vibration: a short buzz when you turn the on-screen bezel.

## Children
Simple ADB is a tool for adults and is not directed at children.
